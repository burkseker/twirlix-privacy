Twirlix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Twirlix ("the game") is a puzzle game published by Burak Seker ("we") in which you match colored balls onto a spinning cluster. This policy explains what the game does with data. It is short because the game runs entirely on your device: it does not connect to the internet, does not ask you to create an account, shows no ads and asks you for nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no internet access, we run no servers and we receive no data from it. We cannot see who plays or how.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following in the device's private app storage and sends none of it anywhere:

  • your record (best score);
  • whether sound and vibration are on;
  • whether you have finished the tutorial.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your own backup; that backup lives in your Google account and never reaches us.

You are in control: Android Settings → Apps → Twirlix → Storage → Clear data resets everything, and uninstalling the game leaves nothing behind.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission, for short buzzes when balls pop. You can turn vibration off in the game's menu.

Links

The More games button in the menu opens our developer page on Google Play. From there on, Google Play's policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the way the game handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
