Twirlix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 (world leaderboard)

Twirlix ("the game") is a puzzle game published by Burak Seker ("we") in which you match colored balls onto a spinning cluster. This policy explains what the game does with data. It is short because the game itself runs entirely on your device, does not ask you to create an account and asks you for nothing. What goes online is the advertising and the world leaderboard described below.

What we collect

Only what the world leaderboard needs, described below. Beyond that we receive no data from the game.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following in the device's private app storage and sends none of it anywhere:

  • your record (best score);
  • whether sound and vibration are on;
  • whether you have finished the tutorial.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your own backup; that backup lives in your Google account and never reaches us.

You are in control: Android Settings → Apps → Twirlix → Storage → Clear data resets everything, and uninstalling the game leaves nothing behind.

World leaderboard

When a game ends, your score is sent to our leaderboard server. What is sent:

  • a random identifier the game makes up on first launch (not tied to your device, your account or you);
  • the nickname shown on the leaderboard (it starts as something like "Oyuncu-1234"; change it in the game under Leaderboard → Change your name);
  • your score, the level reached, how long the game lasted, the mode (classic or daily game) and whether it is Android or iOS;
  • the country your connection comes from. Cloudflare, which hosts the server, derives it from the connection's IP address; we do not store your IP address.

Your nickname and scores are public on the leaderboard; your identifier is not. The data is kept on Cloudflare's infrastructure. To have your records deleted, write to brkskr01@gmail.com with your nickname.

Ads

When you start again after a game ends, a full-screen ad is shown every few games. The ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google. To do this, the AdMob software inside the game collects and processes data about the device: its advertising ID, IP address, the approximate location derived from it, device and operating system details, and your interactions with the ads.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form is shown when the game first opens, and no ad is requested until you have answered it.

That processing is Google's, not ours; we only see aggregate counts of the ads shown. How Google uses this data is explained at:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions

The game uses the vibration permission for short buzzes when balls pop; you can turn vibration off in the game's menu. The internet permission is for the ads and the leaderboard; the advertising ID permission is only for the ads.

Links

The More games button in the menu opens our developer page on Google Play. From there on, Google Play's policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the way the game handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact
