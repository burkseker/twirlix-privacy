Twirlix: delete your data

Twirlix by Burak Seker, on Google Play and the App Store

Twirlix has no accounts. The only data that leaves your device is your entry on the world leaderboard. You can have it deleted at any time.

How to ask for deletion

  • In the game, open Leaderboard and note the nickname shown for you (for example "Oyuncu-1234").
  • Email brkskr01@gmail.com with the subject "Twirlix data deletion" and that nickname. If you can, mention roughly when you last played and your best score, so we find the right record.
  • We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your leaderboard identifier (the random ID, or your Google Play Games player ID if you signed in);
  • your nickname, platform and country;
  • all your scores, on every board (all time, weekly, country and daily).

Nothing is kept afterwards: there are no backups or extra retention periods. If you play again later, the game starts a new, empty entry.

Data on your phone

Your record, settings, unlocks and saved game stay on your device and never reach us. Clear the app's data or uninstall the game to remove them. Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's privacy policy.
